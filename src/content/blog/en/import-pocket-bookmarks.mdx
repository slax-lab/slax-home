Pocket has closed, but your saved links don't have to go with it. If you kept your Pocket export file, you can import Pocket bookmarks into Slax Reader with one upload. Importing is free, and Slax Reader does the slow part for you in the background.

## Before you start

- **Your Pocket export file.** It is a ZIP that holds one or more CSV files named like `part_000000.csv`. Pocket stopped saving on July 8, 2025, and [its last export deadline was November 12, 2025](https://www.vice.com/en/article/mozilla-pocket-gets-one-month-reprieve/), so you can't get a new one. Search your Downloads folder, email and cloud storage for "pocket".
- **A Slax Reader account.** [Sign up or log in](https://r.slax.com).
- **Leave the ZIP closed.** Don't unzip it. Slax Reader opens it for you.

## Import your Pocket export in five steps

1. **Open Settings.** Log in at [r.slax.com](https://r.slax.com), move your mouse over your avatar and click **Setting**.
2. **Go to Data.** Click **Data** in the settings navigation. Find the card called **Import third-party data**.
3. **Click the Pocket button.** Press **Import third-party data Pocket**. Your file picker opens.
4. **Choose your ZIP.** You'll see "Unzipping file..." and then "Uploading file...". When it finishes you get the message **pocket data imported successfully, please wait a moment**. That means the file arrived. Your links are saved next, in the background.
5. **Check the progress.** Click **View import progress**. The **Import progress** window shows the Status as Pending, then Processing with a percentage, then **Complete**. Then open your Inbox.

You can close the window and keep reading while it runs. A few hundred links finish quickly. Thousands take longer, because Slax Reader fetches every page again to save a fresh copy.

## What moves over

| From Pocket | In Slax Reader |
|---|---|
| Link and title | Saved bookmark |
| Tags | Tags |
| Archived items | Still archived |
| Original saved date | Not kept; links are dated from the day you import them |
| Highlights and notes | Not included, so keep your export file |

A Pocket export lists links and details, not the article text. A page that has since been deleted from the web can't be fetched again.

## If some links fail

Some always will: deleted pages, sites that block automated visits, links behind a login.

1. In **Import progress**, click the number in the **Failed** column.
2. The **Import Failures** window lists them. Open the original page to see if it still exists.
3. If it does, save it again with the [Slax Reader browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/slax-reader/gdnhaajlomjkhahnmiijphnodkcfikfd).
4. Click **Batch Delete** (after ticking items) or **Delete All** to clear the list.

**Nothing happens after I pick the file?** Make sure it is the original ZIP and contains a file like `part_000000.csv`. A renamed or rebuilt ZIP won't match.

## FAQ

### Is importing free?
Yes. You can import Pocket bookmarks into Slax Reader for free.

### Can I still export from Pocket?
No. The export window closed on November 12, 2025. You need a file you saved earlier.
