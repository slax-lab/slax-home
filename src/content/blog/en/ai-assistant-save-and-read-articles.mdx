Your AI assistant is great at finding good articles. The problem is what happens next: it has nowhere to keep them. Close the chat and the links are gone, and next week it can't remember what you read together.

Connect Slax Reader and that changes. Your assistant can save links to your library, look through what you've saved, and read any saved article in full, even if the original page has since been taken down.

## Things you can say to your AI

- **"Save these links to my Slax Reader."** They land in your Inbox, each with a full copy of the page.
- **"What did I save this week about pricing?"** Your assistant looks through your library and tells you which articles are worth reading first.
- **"Use the article I saved this morning to help me write this."** It reads the full article straight from your library, so you don't have to paste anything in.

You keep reading in Slax Reader as usual, on the web or your phone. Your AI just gets a way in.

## Set it up in two minutes

You don't need to type any commands yourself. Your AI does the installing.

1. **Get your key.** In Slax Reader, open **Settings → Skills + CLI** and click **Generate and copy**.
2. **Ask your AI to install it.** Paste this into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor:

   > Help me install Slax Reader CLI: https://github.com/slax-lab/slax-reader/blob/dev/docs/cli/ai-agent-installation-guide.md

3. **Paste your key** when it asks, then restart your assistant. Try one of the sentences above.

Your key works like a password for your library, so keep it to yourself.
