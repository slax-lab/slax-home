Reading on your phone usually happens in small gaps: a few minutes in an elevator, on the subway, in a queue. So we built this Slax Reader update around one goal: pick up your phone and keep reading right away.

The new app shows what's already on your phone first and syncs in the background. Articles save to your device automatically, so they're there without a connection. Search has been rebuilt, and there are new ways to save. Here's what's new and how to use it.

## Open it and read

The app used to make you wait for sync before you saw much. Now your library and avatars live on your device, so they appear as soon as you open the app, while sync runs in the background.

Articles work the same way. A downloaded article opens straight at the spot where you left off, with no blank flash and no waiting on outside images.

**How to use it:** there's nothing to set up. When "Continue reading" appears at the bottom of your list, tap it to go back to the article you were reading.

## Read without a connection

The app downloads article text, images and PDFs in the background, so you can keep reading on the subway or a plane. Links you add while offline aren't lost either: they appear in your list right away and finish saving once you're back online.

**How to use it:**

1. Open the sidebar and go to Settings.
2. Under the offline cache setting, choose how many articles to keep on your phone. Past that limit, the oldest cached articles are removed from your device. Your permanent snapshots in the cloud aren't affected.
3. To save space, turn off image downloads.
4. Use Clear Cache to see how much space is in use and remove what you don't need. Automatically cached text and images aren't affected.

## A new search

Search is now its own full-screen page. As you type, it finds matching articles, collections and RSS items on your device. Submit the search and it also looks through the full text of your saved articles, with the site and save date shown on each result.

**How to use it:**

1. Tap the search icon next to the Add button at the top of your Inbox.
2. Start typing, and local results appear immediately.
3. To filter by tag, type `#` and pick one, or choose from the tag picker at the bottom.
4. Tap Search to look through the full text of your saved articles too.

## More ways to save

When you find something worth reading, you can now save it in several ways:

- **Share sheet:** tap Share in Safari, Chrome or any other app, then choose Slax Reader.
- **Paste a link:** tap Add in the app, paste the link and confirm.
- **Siri and Shortcuts (iOS):** say "Save to Slax Reader" to Siri, or use the Save link action in the Shortcuts app.

On iOS, you can also find articles without opening the app. System search shows up to 500 of your most recently saved articles, and tapping one opens it directly. This needs iOS 18 or later. Slax Reader only gives the system titles and site names; article text, notes and full links stay in the app. To turn it off, go to Settings and disable showing saved articles in system search.

## PDFs, RSS and YouTube on your phone

All three are in Labs, so they're off by default. Turn them on and they're ready to use.

**PDF:**

1. Turn on the PDF lab in Settings.
2. Tap Add and choose to import a PDF file. It's one file at a time, up to 50 MiB, and uploading needs a connection.
3. Open the PDF to read, highlight and comment. Downloaded PDFs work offline too.

**RSS:**

1. Turn on the RSS lab in Settings.
2. In RSS feeds, tap Add and enter an RSS or Atom address.
3. Switch between your feeds and save anything you want to read closely to your Inbox.

**YouTube:**

1. On the web, go to Settings → Labs and turn on YouTube videos. For now, this can only be turned on from the web.
2. Share a YouTube link to Slax Reader, the same way you save an article.
3. Open the video, tap More in the top right, then tap Transcript. The current line is highlighted as the video plays, and tapping any line jumps to it.

## Small things that make it nicer

- Swipe an article in your list to star or archive it.
- Tap an image in an article to zoom in, save it to your photos or share it.
- Highlights, comments, AI overviews and outlines all work in the app, and your notes sync with the web.
- If something goes wrong, tell us through Feedback in the sidebar. Attaching recent logs is up to you, and that option is off by default.
