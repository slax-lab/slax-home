After the Slax extension is pinned to your browser, you can click the icon to save it when you see a page you want to read later.

</ImageText>

<ImageText src="/reader/images/blog/quick-save-with-the-slax-extension/inbox-menu-en.png" alt="Right-click menu on the Slax extension icon, showing the Slax Inbox option" side="right">

Right-click the extension icon in the top-right corner and select "Slax Inbox ↗︎" to return to your saved items.

In the Slax Reader snapshot page, select any text to highlight it.

</ImageText>

Slax Reader is also available as an app on your phone.
