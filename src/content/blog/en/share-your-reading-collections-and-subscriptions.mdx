A lot of good reading reaches us through other people. A friend who always sends the one article worth your evening. A colleague whose links you open without thinking. A newsletter writer whose taste you trust more than any algorithm.

Slax Reader now has a few simple ways to be that person for others, and to follow the people who are that person for you. Nothing to set up from scratch. It builds on two things you already do: you **save** articles to your library, and you **star** the ones that are really worth reading.

## Your stars become a reading list

Saving an article just keeps it for later. Starring it says "this one is good." A **collection** turns your starred articles into a public page that anyone can open. It only shows what you star, never the rest of your Inbox.

To start one, open **Starred** in your library on the web. You'll see a small card: "Your stars can help others discover great content." Click **Enable collection**, and your page goes live.

From then on, it looks after itself:

- **Everything you star is added automatically**, including the articles you starred before.
- **Readers see your picks grouped by month**, with how many highlights you made in each one.
- **You get one link to share.** Click **Share collection** to copy it or post it to X.

Want to give it a name? Open **Manage collection** to change the title and add a short description. By default it's called "*Your name*'s Reading Picks".

Changed your mind? **Close collection** in the same place takes the page offline. Your stars stay exactly as they are, and you can reopen it whenever you like.

## Subscribe to someone else's picks

When someone shares their collection link with you, open it and click **Subscribe**. That's it. Whenever they star a new article, it appears in your Slax Reader, without you checking their page.

You'll find them at the top of your Inbox: a row of small avatars, one for your own Inbox and one for each collection you follow. Their picks stay separate from the articles you saved yourself. A dot means there's something new. Tap an avatar to see that person's picks, with how many highlights they made and a comment they left, so you know why they starred it before you open it. Open one, and you read it with their highlights and comments too, unless they chose to hide them.

It works on your phone as well as on the web. And if a collection stops being useful, **Unsubscribe** removes it from your Inbox.

## Save what you like from other people

When you open an article from someone's collection, or from a link they sent you, and it's one you want to keep, click **Save for later**. It goes into your own library, next to everything you saved yourself, and it stays there even if they later unstar it or close their collection. From there it's yours: highlight it, add comments, star it, and it can end up in your own collection too.

That's how a good article travels. One person stars it, a friend saves it, and the friend's subscribers find it next.

## Share one article, with your highlights

Sometimes you don't want to share a whole list. You just want to send one article and say "read the part I highlighted."

Open the article on the web and click **Share**. You can **copy the link** or **share it to X**. The person who opens it reads the clean saved page, even if they don't use Slax Reader. If they do, they can click **Save for later** to keep their own copy.

In the same menu there's a **Show highlights** checkbox. When it's ticked, they also see your highlights and the comments you wrote on them, right in the article. When it's not, they get the article alone. It's ticked by default, so untick it before you copy the link if your highlights are just for you.

## A few ways people use it

- **Newsletter writers** put their collection link at the bottom of each issue. Readers who subscribe get the picks as they happen, not once a week.
- **Teams** keep one shared reading list for the industry news everyone should know about, instead of a busy chat channel where links get buried.
- **Teachers and study groups** share an article with highlights showing, so everyone reads the same key passages.
- **Friends** follow each other's collections, the way you'd swap book recommendations.

## FAQ

### Does my collection share my whole Inbox?
No. It only shows articles you star. Articles you saved but didn't star stay private.

### Is my collection public?
Only if you choose to make it public. Nothing is shared until you click **Enable collection**. After that, anyone with the link can see the articles you star, and you can close it again at any time. A single article is only seen by the people you send its link to.

### Can I choose which starred articles go into my collection?
Your collection shows everything you star. If you'd rather keep an article out, unstar it.

### What do my subscribers see if I close my collection?
They see that it's closed, and nothing new arrives. If you reopen it, new picks start appearing in their Inbox again.

### Can I change my collection link?
No. The link is made for you and stays the same, so the links you've already shared keep working.
