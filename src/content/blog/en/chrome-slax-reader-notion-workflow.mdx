Every day, you find useful things online:

- An article you want to read, but it's too long
- A tool you might need later, but no time to read through its tutorial
- A reference for your next piece of work, but by the time you need it, you won't remember where you saw it

Slax Reader is built for exactly these moments. No second brain to set up: just the tools you already use and a few simple habits.

> **Chrome helps you discover. Slax Reader helps you keep the content for reading and understanding. Your work tool (Notion, Google Docs, Word, or Excel) helps you turn ideas into work.**

## The complete workflow

| Stage | Your question | Best tool | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | What did I find? | Google Chrome | Browse the web and find useful content |
| 2. Save and read | What deserves my time? | Slax Reader | Save, read, search, highlight, AI outline, chat, understand and organize |
| 3. Apply and create | How will I use it? | Notion, Google Docs, Word, or Excel | Turn important ideas into notes, projects, and plans |

## If I already use Google Chrome, do I need Slax Reader?

Google Chrome is where you browse, discover, and open webpages. Chrome's Reading List saves the link, but the page itself still lives on someone else's website. As a read-later app, Slax Reader adds a dedicated reading layer for content you want to return to later.

- **Read it properly.** Save an article from Chrome, read it in a clean format, and continue on any device.
- **Find it again.** Organize with tags and stars, and search the full saved text.
- **Keep what matters.** Highlight important passages and add comments.
- **Get through long articles faster.** Slax Reader Pro can outline an article or answer questions about it.

Chrome gives you access to the page; Slax Reader gives that page a lasting place in your reading library.

## If I already use Notion, do I need Slax Reader?

Notion is built for writing notes, managing projects, creating databases, and turning ideas into structured work. You can clip pages into it with the Notion Web Clipper, but clipping isn't reading. Slax Reader acts as a reading inbox, adding the filtering and understanding layer that comes before those steps.

- **A pause that works like a filter.** Save freely, read when you're ready, and keep only what's useful. Notion only receives content that has already proven useful.
- **Your Notion stays lean.** Articles don't pile up in your workspace or eat into its storage. Slax Reader captures and stores full pages for you, unlimited and free.
- **Fast, because it's already saved.** Slax Reader keeps a full copy of every page, so it opens right away instead of waiting for the original website to load.
- **Always there.** News stories expire, and sites that nobody maintains go offline. Once a page is in Slax Reader, it stays.

Once the ideas are clear, move them into Notion. Not a Notion user? The same works for Google Docs, Word, or Excel.

## A practical example: preparing a presentation on AI tools

**In Chrome**
- Search for technical articles, user reviews, similar tools, YouTube videos, and podcasts.
- Click **Save** with the [Slax Reader extension](/blog/quick-save-with-the-slax-extension/) on each page that looks useful, or paste the link into Slax Reader on the web or in the app.

**In Slax Reader**
- Read through what you saved anytime, on your phone or laptop. Keep the useful ones and archive the rest. This pause works as your filter.
- Highlight key sentences and add comments.
- Get through long articles in a few minutes: skim them yourself, or let AI sum them up for you. YouTube videos (beta) and podcasts are turned into text, so the same works for them too.
- Add tags such as `AI`, `AI tools presentation`, or `research`. Next time you come back to Slax Reader, open a tag to see everything you saved on that topic in one place.
- When you need something again, search by any keyword you remember: in the browser, in the phone app, or with Spotlight or Siri on iPhone.

**In Notion (or Google Docs, Word, Excel)**
- Write the outline, talking points, and notes for your team.
- Paste the Slax Reader links as your sources.

If Obsidian or a second-brain setup feels like too much work before you've even read anything, start simpler: save it, read it, tag it in Slax Reader. Build the system later, if you still need one.
